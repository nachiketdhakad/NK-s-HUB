Help with Orders

How do I place an order?▾
Browse the Menu page, tap "Add to Cart" on the dishes you want, then go to Cart and click Checkout. Fill in your delivery address and payment method to place the order.
Can I edit my order after placing it?▾
Yes — as long as your order status is "Placed", go to My Orders → open the order → Edit Delivery Details to update your phone number or address. Once it moves to "On the Way" it can no longer be edited.
How do I cancel my order?▾
Go to My Orders → open the order you want to cancel → click "Cancel Order" and confirm. Orders can only be cancelled while their status is still "Placed".
How can I track my order?▾
Open My Orders from the navbar and click on any order to see its live status — Placed, On the Way, or Delivered.

Payments & Refunds

What payment methods are accepted?▾
We accept UPI (Scan & Pay), Credit/Debit Cards, and Cash on Delivery. Cash on Delivery is available only for orders under ₹1000.
My payment failed but money got deducted, what now?▾
If a payment is deducted but the order doesn't show up in My Orders, please wait a few minutes for it to reflect. If it still doesn't appear, contact us at nachiketdhakad@gmail.com with your payment screenshot and we'll resolve it quickly.
How do I apply a coupon code?▾
At Checkout, click on "Apply Coupon" in the order summary, enter your code, and hit Apply. The discount will reflect in your final bill immediately.
How long do refunds take?▾
For cancelled prepaid orders, refunds are usually processed within 5-7 business days back to your original payment method.

Delivery & Address

Which areas do you deliver to?▾
We currently deliver to Vijay Nagar, Palasia, Sudama Nagar, Rajendra Nagar and Malviya Nagar in Indore. Enter your pincode at checkout to check availability instantly.
How do I add or change my delivery address?▾
Go to My Account → Addresses, or click "Change Address" during checkout. You can save up to 4 addresses and mark one as active.
How long does delivery usually take?▾
Delivery typically takes 30-45 minutes depending on your distance from the nearest outlet and current order volume.

Account & Profile

How do I edit my profile details?▾
Go to My Account → Edit Profile to update your username, email, or phone number.
How does the Favourites list work?▾
Tap the heart icon on any dish's page to save it to your Favourites. View or remove them anytime from My Account → Favourites.
I forgot my password, what do I do?▾
Please email us at nachiketdhakad@gmail.com with your registered username or phone number and we'll help you regain access.

General Issues

The app/website isn't loading properly▾
Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. If the issue persists, please reach out to us with a screenshot of the problem.
I want to give feedback about my order▾
You can leave a rating and review on any dish's page after ordering, or write to us directly via the Contact Us page.
How do I report a safety concern?▾
For urgent safety issues regarding food quality or delivery, email us immediately at nachiketdhakad@gmail.com with details and we'll prioritize your case.

Still need help?

Mail us at nachiketdhakad@gmail.com — we usually respond within 24 hours.
